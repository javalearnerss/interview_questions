Completion Rule

For each pattern:

1. Solve 1 question by watching or reading explanation.
2. Solve 1 similar question with hints.
3. Solve 2-3 questions completely alone.
4. Re-solve important questions after 2-3 days.

If you can solve traversal, height, diameter, path sum, LCA, right view, validate BST, kth smallest BST, tree construction, and balanced tree without help, your tree foundation is strong.
