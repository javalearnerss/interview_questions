🎯 How to Use These Questions

Prepare each scenario using this framework:

1. Root cause: What is likely failing?
2. Investigation: Which AWS resources, logs, metrics, and Kafka settings would you inspect?
3. Resolution: What would you change?
4. Prevention: How would you prevent recurrence?
5. Trade-offs: How would your decision affect security, availability, throughput, latency, and cost?

Recommended preparation sequence

Since your plan is to connect through ECS first and then EKS, follow this order:

First: Categories 1–4 — networking, IAM, security, and ECS deployment.

Next: Categories 5–7 — performance, monitoring, and reliability.

Finally: Category 8 — extending the architecture to EKS and planning migration.

This gives you a practical learning path: implement the architecture, validate it, troubleshoot realistic failures, and then prepare to explain your decisions at a senior engineering interview.
