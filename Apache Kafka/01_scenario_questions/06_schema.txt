🎯 Schema Registry Scenario-Based Interview Questions

FOUNDATION (3 questions):
1. What is schema evolution and why is it needed in Kafka?
2. Explain backward compatibility, forward compatibility, and full compatibility with examples.
3. What is Schema Registry and what problems does it solve?

PRODUCER-CONSUMER ISSUES (4 questions):
4. Producer deploys a schema change and consumers crash. What went wrong?
5. Old consumers break after a new field is added. Why?
6. A backward-compatible change still causes issues. Why?
7. A required field is added without a default value. What happens to old messages during replay?

SCHEMA REGISTRY AVAILABILITY (2 questions):
8. Schema Registry is down — what happens to producers and consumers?
9. What's the impact of Schema Registry caching on system performance?

SAFE EVOLUTION (3 questions):
10. Different teams evolve schemas independently. How do you prevent outages?
11. How do you safely remove a field from events without breaking consumers?
12. You change the data type: `amount: double` → `amount: string`. How do you handle this in production?

RECOVERY & DEBUGGING (2 questions):
13. A schema change corrupts downstream data. How do you recover?
14. Replay of old events fails due to schema mismatch. Why?

TESTING & DEPLOYMENT (1 question):
15. How do you test schema changes before production and what's the safe deployment order?
